# Experience Record: Marcus Vance High-Volume Sales Strategies (EXP-001)

## Overview of Sales Experience for Marcus Vance

Marcus Vance achieved 54 sales of the Porsche 911 Carrera S and 60 sales of the Audi e-tron GT Quattro at the London Central branch during the 2024 calendar year (1 January–31 December 2024).

## Primary Reasons for Marcus Vance Sales Success on Porsche 911 Carrera S

Marcus Vance sold 54 Porsche 911 Carrera S units during the 2024 calendar year. The following practices supported those sales:
- Qualified buyers on intended use, preferred specification, and delivery timing before arranging a viewing, keeping conversations focused on a high-value sports-car purchase.
- Used side-by-side specification and condition discussions to explain the Carrera S configuration, then tailored the test drive to steering response, acceleration, and everyday usability.
- Presented itemised part-exchange and finance options in writing, with a clear breakdown of deposit, monthly payment, and any optional products.

## Primary Reasons for Marcus Vance Sales Success on Audi e-tron GT Quattro

Marcus Vance sold 60 Audi e-tron GT Quattro units during the 2024 calendar year. The following practices supported those sales:
- Started with a charging and journey-needs check, then demonstrated the e-tron GT’s grand-touring comfort and dual-motor quattro capability on a suitable route.
- Explained battery range as an estimate affected by speed, temperature, and driving conditions; helped buyers identify home, workplace, and public charging options.
- Used a repeatable walk-through of charging-port operation, regenerative-braking settings, cabin technology, and ownership costs to build confidence before handover.
