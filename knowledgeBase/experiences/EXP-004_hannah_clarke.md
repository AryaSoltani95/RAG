# Experience Record: Hannah Clarke High-Volume Sales Strategies (EXP-004)

## Overview of Sales Experience for Hannah Clarke

Hannah Clarke achieved 64 sales of the BMW 330e M Sport and 90 sales of the Toyota Yaris Cross Hybrid at the Leeds South branch during the 2024 calendar year (1 January–31 December 2024).

## Primary Reasons for Hannah Clarke Sales Success on BMW 330e M Sport

Hannah Clarke sold 64 BMW 330e M Sport units during the 2024 calendar year. The following practices supported those sales:
- Asked company-car and private buyers about commute length, charging access, and typical trip mix before explaining how the plug-in hybrid can be used.
- Demonstrated the M Sport cabin and controls, and explained the distinction between electric driving when charged and hybrid operation when battery charge is depleted.
- Discussed charging routines and customer-specific running-cost assumptions transparently, then compared finance and part-exchange figures without treating estimated savings as guaranteed.

## Primary Reasons for Hannah Clarke Sales Success on Toyota Yaris Cross Hybrid

Hannah Clarke sold 90 Toyota Yaris Cross Hybrid units during the 2024 calendar year. The following practices supported those sales:
- Focused the walk-around on the Yaris Cross Hybrid’s compact footprint, elevated seating position, cabin storage, and boot to address urban and small-family needs.
- Explained self-charging hybrid operation and regenerative braking, distinguishing it from a plug-in model and avoiding promises of a fixed fuel figure.
- Used a practical test-drive route to demonstrate visibility and low-speed manoeuvring, then provided a written quote with clearly itemised options.
