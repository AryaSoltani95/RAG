# Experience Record: Claire Thornton High-Volume Sales Strategies (EXP-002)

## Overview of Sales Experience for Claire Thornton

Claire Thornton achieved 48 sales of the Land Rover Defender 110 and 72 sales of the Hyundai Tucson Hybrid at the Manchester Central branch during the 2024 calendar year (1 January–31 December 2024).

## Primary Reasons for Claire Thornton Sales Success on Land Rover Defender 110

Claire Thornton sold 48 Land Rover Defender 110 units during the 2024 calendar year. The following practices supported those sales:
- Established whether customers needed seven-seat flexibility, cargo space, towing capability, or genuine off-road use, then showed how the Defender 110’s layout matched their priorities.
- Demonstrated the vehicle’s configurable cabin, practical access, and relevant Terrain Response settings in a controlled setting, avoiding claims that a road test proved off-road capability.
- Discussed running costs, vehicle dimensions, and parking needs alongside finance and part-exchange figures so buyers could weigh capability against daily practicality.

## Primary Reasons for Claire Thornton Sales Success on Hyundai Tucson Hybrid

Claire Thornton sold 72 Hyundai Tucson Hybrid units during the 2024 calendar year. The following practices supported those sales:
- Matched the Tucson Hybrid’s cabin, boot, and driver-assistance features to family and commuting needs during a structured walk-around.
- Explained hybrid operation in plain language, including regenerative braking and how the petrol engine and electric motor work together, without promising a fixed fuel economy.
- Compared customer-specific finance and trade-in options and followed up with a written feature summary, helping households make a considered decision.
