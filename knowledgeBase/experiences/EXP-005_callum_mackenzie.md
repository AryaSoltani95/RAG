# Experience Record: Callum Mackenzie High-Volume Sales Strategies (EXP-005)

## Overview of Sales Experience for Callum Mackenzie

Callum Mackenzie achieved 51 sales of the Land Rover Defender 110 and 78 sales of the Skoda Octavia Estate SE L at the Edinburgh City branch during the 2024 calendar year (1 January–31 December 2024).

## Primary Reasons for Callum Mackenzie Sales Success on Land Rover Defender 110

Callum Mackenzie sold 51 Land Rover Defender 110 units during the 2024 calendar year. The following practices supported those sales:
- Opened with questions about passenger numbers, outdoor activities, towing, and parking so that the Defender 110’s size and capability could be judged against real needs.
- Showed cabin flexibility, access, and driver-selectable terrain settings; kept capability explanations tied to the customer’s intended use and vehicle specification.
- Compared the higher-capability vehicle’s likely ownership demands with alternatives, clearly detailing finance, trade-in, and practical dimensions before commitment.

## Primary Reasons for Callum Mackenzie Sales Success on Skoda Octavia Estate SE L

Callum Mackenzie sold 78 Skoda Octavia Estate SE L units during the 2024 calendar year. The following practices supported those sales:
- Matched the Octavia Estate’s load space, rear-seat room, and SE L equipment to family, work, and leisure requirements using a customer’s typical luggage as a reference.
- Demonstrated the tailgate, rear access, infotainment, and relevant parking or driver-assistance features fitted to the specific vehicle.
- Made the estate-versus-SUV choice concrete with a test-drive and written comparison of vehicle price, part exchange, and finance terms.
