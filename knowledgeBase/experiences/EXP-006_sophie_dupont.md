# Experience Record: Sophie Dupont High-Volume Sales Strategies (EXP-006)

## Overview of Sales Experience for Sophie Dupont

Sophie Dupont achieved 66 sales of the Tesla Model Y Long Range and 82 sales of the Toyota Yaris Cross Hybrid at the Bristol Harbourside branch during the 2024 calendar year (1 January–31 December 2024).

## Primary Reasons for Sophie Dupont Sales Success on Tesla Model Y Long Range

Sophie Dupont sold 66 Tesla Model Y Long Range units during the 2024 calendar year. The following practices supported those sales:
- Used a route-and-charging questionnaire to check daily mileage, longer journeys, and access to home or workplace charging before recommending the Model Y Long Range.
- Demonstrated cabin and cargo flexibility, touchscreen essentials, and charging-port use, then took buyers on a route suited to assessing comfort and visibility.
- Explained that actual range varies with weather, speed, and use; provided a realistic charging-planning discussion and transparent ownership-cost assumptions.

## Primary Reasons for Sophie Dupont Sales Success on Toyota Yaris Cross Hybrid

Sophie Dupont sold 82 Toyota Yaris Cross Hybrid units during the 2024 calendar year. The following practices supported those sales:
- Connected the Yaris Cross Hybrid’s dimensions, seating position, and boot layout to customers’ city parking, commuting, and family errands.
- Explained the self-charging hybrid system, including energy recovery during deceleration, and clarified that it does not require plugging in.
- Used a short urban test-drive to show manoeuvrability and visibility, followed by a tailored written quote and prompt CRM follow-up.
