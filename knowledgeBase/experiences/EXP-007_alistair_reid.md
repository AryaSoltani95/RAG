# Experience Record: Alistair Reid High-Volume Sales Strategies (EXP-007)

## Overview of Sales Experience for Alistair Reid

Alistair Reid achieved 110 sales of the Ford Transit Custom Leader and 58 sales of the Skoda Octavia Estate SE L at the Glasgow West branch during the 2024 calendar year (1 January–31 December 2024).

## Primary Reasons for Alistair Reid Sales Success on Ford Transit Custom Leader

Alistair Reid sold 110 Ford Transit Custom Leader units during the 2024 calendar year. The following practices supported those sales:
- Qualified tradespeople and fleet buyers on payload needs, typical equipment, mileage, and crew requirements before matching them to the Transit Custom Leader configuration.
- Demonstrated load-area access, usable space, cab storage, and practical loading considerations; checked the exact vehicle specification rather than assuming every van had identical equipment.
- Prepared clear business-use quotations with trade-in, finance, and delivery details, and followed up with decision-makers to keep fleet enquiries moving.

## Primary Reasons for Alistair Reid Sales Success on Skoda Octavia Estate SE L

Alistair Reid sold 58 Skoda Octavia Estate SE L units during the 2024 calendar year. The following practices supported those sales:
- Showed how the Octavia Estate’s load area and passenger space can suit mixed work-and-family use, using customers’ actual kit or luggage dimensions where available.
- Walked through SE L equipment and everyday controls in the specific car, then used a practical drive to assess ride comfort and visibility.
- Presented the estate as a distinct alternative to a van or SUV, with transparent purchase and finance figures and a documented follow-up.
