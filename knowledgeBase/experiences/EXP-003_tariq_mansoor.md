# Experience Record: Tariq Mansoor High-Volume Sales Strategies (EXP-003)

## Overview of Sales Experience for Tariq Mansoor

Tariq Mansoor achieved 85 sales of the Tesla Model Y Long Range and 42 sales of the Audi e-tron GT Quattro at the Birmingham North branch during the 2024 calendar year (1 January–31 December 2024).

## Primary Reasons for Tariq Mansoor Sales Success on Tesla Model Y Long Range

Tariq Mansoor sold 85 Tesla Model Y Long Range units during the 2024 calendar year. The following practices supported those sales:
- Mapped each buyer’s regular routes and charging access before discussing the Model Y Long Range, using those details to assess whether an EV suited their routine.
- Demonstrated the spacious cabin, flexible load area, infotainment, and driver-assistance controls, while making clear that drivers remain responsible for supervision.
- Set expectations about real-world range and charging time, then helped customers plan home or public charging and compare a tailored ownership-cost estimate.

## Primary Reasons for Tariq Mansoor Sales Success on Audi e-tron GT Quattro

Tariq Mansoor sold 42 Audi e-tron GT Quattro units during the 2024 calendar year. The following practices supported those sales:
- Positioned the e-tron GT Quattro as an electric grand tourer by demonstrating cabin comfort, performance features, and the quattro drivetrain on a planned test-drive route.
- Walked through charging, regenerative braking, and the vehicle’s displays; discussed range as variable rather than a guaranteed distance.
- Presented a clear comparison of specification, part-exchange value, and finance choices, then arranged follow-up to address questions after the test drive.
