# Experience Record: Elena Rostova High-Volume Sales Strategies (EXP-010)

## Overview of Sales Experience for Elena Rostova

Elena Rostova achieved 95 sales of the Ford Transit Custom Leader and 47 sales of the BMW 330e M Sport at the Cambridge North branch during the 2024 calendar year (1 January–31 December 2024).

## Primary Reasons for Elena Rostova Sales Success on Ford Transit Custom Leader

Elena Rostova sold 95 Ford Transit Custom Leader units during the 2024 calendar year. The following practices supported those sales:
- Asked business customers about payload, tools, passenger needs, and regular routes, then confirmed the Transit Custom Leader’s exact configuration against those requirements.
- Demonstrated cab storage, access to the load area, and practical loading points; explained dimensions and equipment in terms of the customer’s actual daily work.
- Issued clear quotations separating vehicle price, trade-in, and finance terms, and maintained timely follow-up with fleet contacts and sole traders.

## Primary Reasons for Elena Rostova Sales Success on BMW 330e M Sport

Elena Rostova sold 47 BMW 330e M Sport units during the 2024 calendar year. The following practices supported those sales:
- Checked commute patterns and charging availability before presenting the 330e M Sport as a plug-in hybrid suited to buyers able to charge regularly.
- Demonstrated M Sport trim and the hybrid system’s displays, explaining that electric range and overall consumption vary with charging, weather, and driving.
- Compared tailored finance and part-exchange choices and documented any running-cost assumptions so customers could assess the car against their own use.
