# Experience Record: Priya Sharma High-Volume Sales Strategies (EXP-008)

## Overview of Sales Experience for Priya Sharma

Priya Sharma achieved 55 sales of the BMW 330e M Sport and 65 sales of the Hyundai Tucson Hybrid at the Newcastle Central branch during the 2024 calendar year (1 January–31 December 2024).

## Primary Reasons for Priya Sharma Sales Success on BMW 330e M Sport

Priya Sharma sold 55 BMW 330e M Sport units during the 2024 calendar year. The following practices supported those sales:
- Identified daily distance, workplace or home charging access, and business-car requirements before explaining the 330e’s plug-in hybrid use case.
- Demonstrated M Sport trim details and the driver displays, showing how to check electric and hybrid operation while explaining the need to charge for regular electric driving.
- Built customer-specific comparisons for finance and running costs, making assumptions explicit and separating estimated benefits from guaranteed costs.

## Primary Reasons for Priya Sharma Sales Success on Hyundai Tucson Hybrid

Priya Sharma sold 65 Hyundai Tucson Hybrid units during the 2024 calendar year. The following practices supported those sales:
- Tailored the Tucson Hybrid walk-around to family space, boot access, seating comfort, and the customer’s preferred driver-assistance features.
- Explained the petrol-electric hybrid system and regenerative braking, clarifying how it differs from a plug-in hybrid and that consumption depends on use.
- Used a representative local test-drive to explore comfort and visibility, then supplied a written comparison and clear next steps for part exchange or finance.
