## TradeAI financing and part-exchange policy
TradeAI presents financing and part exchange as optional routes to purchase, not prerequisites for buying a vehicle. TradeAI staff establish customer needs, explain material terms in plain language, and provide current approved documents. TradeAI must not imply that credit is guaranteed, that an indicative quotation is an offer, or that a part-exchange value is final before the stated checks are complete.

## TradeAI finance introductions and customer safeguards
TradeAI offers or introduces only products and activities within its current permissions, appointed-representative arrangements, and approved scripts. TradeAI staff complete required conduct training, obtain consent before sharing personal data, and use only authorized lender systems. TradeAI explains the lender’s identity, representative capacity, relevant fees or commission disclosures, key costs, deposit, term, APR where applicable, optional products, and consequences of missed payments using the lender’s current documents. TradeAI does not give unqualified financial advice or pressure a customer to borrow.

## TradeAI affordability, suitability, and records
TradeAI follows the applicable lender and regulatory process for customer information, vulnerability, affordability, eligibility, and decisioning. TradeAI staff allow customers time to review terms and encourage questions. TradeAI records the customer’s preferences, disclosures, consent, documents supplied, and outcome in approved systems. TradeAI does not coach an applicant to alter information, submit an application without authority, or promise a lender decision.

## TradeAI part-exchange valuation
TradeAI values a part exchange using identity and provenance checks, inspection findings, mileage, specification, service and ownership history, market evidence, outstanding finance, and preparation or transport costs. TradeAI’s supplied staff context refers to a TradingAI Price Index used to explain trade-in and retail pricing; TradeAI staff should use the current approved index and explain that it is one input, not a guaranteed market value. TradeAI gives a written valuation with assumptions, expiry or reinspection conditions, and any required settlement process.

## TradeAI settlement and changes
TradeAI verifies vehicle registration, VIN, keeper authority, title, outstanding finance, and any relevant restrictions before accepting a trade-in. TradeAI does not settle a third-party finance balance without verified figures and authorized payment arrangements. TradeAI revises an offer only when disclosed assumptions prove materially inaccurate or vehicle condition changes, and explains the evidence and customer options. TradeAI separates agreed vehicle price, part-exchange allowance, finance settlement, deposit, and balance in the order documentation.

## TradeAI complaints and governance
TradeAI routes finance complaints through the appropriate regulated complaints process and part-exchange disputes through customer care. TradeAI monitors cancellation, decline, complaint, and valuation adjustment trends for unfair outcomes. TradeAI managers review scripts and disclosures whenever products, lenders, permissions, or law change.
