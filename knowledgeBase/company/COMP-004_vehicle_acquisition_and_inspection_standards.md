## TradeAI vehicle acquisition and inspection standards
TradeAI acquires vehicles only through approved channels and with documented authority, identity, provenance, title, and payment checks. TradeAI may source from private sellers, part exchange, fleets, leasing companies, auctions, or approved trade suppliers. TradeAI staff record source, seller representations, acquisition price, supporting documents, vehicle identifiers, keys, and custody transfer in the approved stock system.

## TradeAI intake and provenance checks
TradeAI verifies VIN and registration consistency, keeper or seller authority, mileage evidence, finance or security interests, theft indicators, write-off or damage history, import status, recalls, and relevant service records using approved sources. TradeAI investigates discrepancies before purchase or sale and escalates suspected fraud, cloned identity, undisclosed finance, or unsafe status. TradeAI never represents an unverified history check as conclusive proof of condition or ownership.

## TradeAI inspection and preparation
TradeAI inspects each vehicle before retail offer using a recorded, risk-based checklist covering safety-critical systems, roadworthiness, tyres, brakes, steering, lights, restraints, structure, leaks, diagnostics, bodywork, interior, accessories, and documentation. TradeAI uses competent personnel and suitable equipment; specialist checks are required where risk, warning indicators, damage, or vehicle technology demands them. TradeAI records defects, measurements where relevant, photographs, repair decisions, parts, and completion sign-off.

## TradeAI condition grading and disclosure
TradeAI assigns condition descriptions under a controlled grading standard and supports each material representation with inspection evidence. TradeAI clearly identifies known defects, cosmetic marks, missing items, modifications, limitations, and outstanding work before agreement. TradeAI makes no unsupported claim such as “perfect,” “accident-free,” or “fully inspected.” TradeAI retains records of advertising, inspection, preparation, and customer disclosures so the represented condition can be reconstructed.

## TradeAI electric and hybrid acquisition controls
TradeAI evaluates high-voltage safety, charging equipment, warning messages, service history, recall status, and battery-health information using competent technicians and approved tools. TradeAI records the method, date, limitations, and result of a battery assessment, without presenting a snapshot as a guarantee of future range or battery life. TradeAI isolates unsafe vehicles and restricts work to trained personnel.

## TradeAI release and audit trail
TradeAI releases a vehicle for sale only after required identity, safety, preparation, documentation, and pricing gates are complete or clearly disclosed. TradeAI blocks sale when a safety-critical defect or legal restriction remains unresolved. TradeAI supervisors sample acquisition and inspection records, investigate repeat defects and supplier issues, and preserve evidence under retention rules.
