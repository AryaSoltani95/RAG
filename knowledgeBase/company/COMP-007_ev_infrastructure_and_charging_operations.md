## TradeAI EV infrastructure and charging operations
TradeAI supports electric and plug-in hybrid vehicle customers with practical information about vehicle charging, battery-health evidence, and available charging facilities. TradeAI distinguishes customer education from installation or electrical engineering advice. TradeAI does not claim that a particular home charger, public network, tariff, range, or charging speed is suitable without checking vehicle compatibility, site conditions, and current provider information.

## TradeAI showroom and hub charging safety
TradeAI sites operate charging equipment only after approval, installation and inspection by competent providers, and inclusion in site risk assessments. TradeAI maintains equipment identification, user instructions, inspection and maintenance records, emergency isolation information, and access controls. TradeAI employees do not use damaged cables or connectors, force incompatible plugs, or undertake unauthorized electrical repairs. TradeAI isolates equipment and reports heat, damage, faults, or suspected electrical hazards immediately.

## TradeAI high-voltage vehicle controls
TradeAI treats high-voltage vehicles and components as specialist hazards. TradeAI permits isolation, diagnosis, repair, or battery handling only by personnel with current competency, suitable personal protective equipment, tools, and approved procedures. TradeAI segregates damaged or suspect EVs and follows emergency and fire-service guidance. TradeAI does not store or charge a suspect battery in an unapproved location.

## TradeAI battery information and customer education
TradeAI explains that displayed range varies with temperature, speed, load, driving style, terrain, battery condition, and auxiliary use. TradeAI records the date, method, equipment, and limitations of any battery-health report and avoids interpreting it as a guarantee of future capacity. TradeAI gives customers the vehicle manufacturer’s relevant charging guidance and explains connector compatibility, charging access, and costs only using current evidence.

## TradeAI charging access and operational planning
TradeAI site managers assess electrical capacity, parking layout, cable trip hazards, accessibility, signage, security, emergency access, and charging demand before changing facilities. TradeAI coordinates any installation with landlords, network operators, insurers, competent contractors, and relevant authorities. TradeAI publishes accurate customer charging availability and any restrictions; TradeAI provides a fallback plan if a charger is unavailable.

## TradeAI environmental and incident controls
TradeAI handles batteries and electrical equipment through approved waste and service providers and retains transfer or maintenance records as required. TradeAI reports charging incidents, near misses, vehicle battery damage, and customer safety concerns through the incident system. TradeAI periodically reviews training, equipment condition, emergency arrangements, and changes in technology or applicable requirements.
