## TradeAI logistics, delivery, and handover protocols
TradeAI moves vehicles between suppliers, hubs, showrooms, workshops, customers, and fleet sites using authorized drivers or approved transport providers. TradeAI verifies vehicle identity, movement authority, insurance, driver competency, route or transporter requirements, and custody responsibilities before dispatch. TradeAI records departure, arrival, mileage, fuel or charge state, keys, documents, and any damage or delay.

## TradeAI pre-delivery readiness
TradeAI confirms that each vehicle has passed required provenance, safety, preparation, documentation, pricing, and release checks before a customer handover. TradeAI resolves safety-critical defects before delivery and clearly documents any permitted outstanding non-critical item, owner, and completion date. TradeAI confirms the customer’s agreed vehicle, specification, price, payment status, registration or transfer arrangements, delivery location, accessibility needs, and time window.

## TradeAI transport and custody
TradeAI uses suitable transport methods and competent providers, with load security and vehicle protection appropriate to the journey. TradeAI communicates collection and delivery expectations, insurance responsibility, and escalation contacts. TradeAI inspects and photographs the vehicle at handover between custodians and records discrepancies immediately. TradeAI does not release a vehicle to an unverified recipient or on an unapproved payment instruction.

## TradeAI customer handover
TradeAI introduces vehicle controls, safety features, known condition points, supplied accessories, service information, warranty documents, and any charging or connectivity basics relevant to the customer. TradeAI gives the customer time to inspect and ask questions and provides required contracts, receipts, finance documents, privacy information, and contact details. TradeAI records date, time, mileage, recipient identity or authority, documents supplied, customer acknowledgment, and any agreed follow-up.

## TradeAI delivery changes and exceptions
TradeAI tells customers promptly about delays, damage, missing items, or changes in specification and obtains agreement to any material variation. TradeAI never substitutes a materially different vehicle without clear customer consent and updated documents. TradeAI escalates failed delivery, payment mismatch, unsafe condition, identity concern, or transport damage to the responsible manager before proceeding. TradeAI logs corrective actions and customer communications.

## TradeAI post-handover support and records
TradeAI follows up through approved customer channels, handles early concerns under the after-sales process, and does not imply that acknowledgment waives statutory rights. TradeAI stores delivery evidence and movement records according to the retention schedule. TradeAI reviews late deliveries, damage, handover omissions, and repeated transport-provider failures for corrective action.
