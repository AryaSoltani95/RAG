## TradeAI B2B fleet and commercial sales framework
TradeAI serves businesses, company-car drivers, fleet operators, and commercial vehicle users through a documented business-to-business process. TradeAI confirms whether the buyer acts as a business or consumer and does not assume every company-named purchase is outside consumer protection. TradeAI provides a named account contact, agreed service scope, and clear escalation route for each material fleet relationship.

## TradeAI discovery and fleet specification
TradeAI gathers the customer’s intended use, vehicle class, payload or seating requirements, annual mileage, duty cycle, access needs, fuel or charging strategy, delivery locations, replacement timing, and budget. TradeAI verifies technical suitability and flags limitations rather than relying solely on a catalogue description. TradeAI offers vehicle-level condition and history documentation and identifies any lead time or availability assumptions.

## TradeAI quotations and contracting
TradeAI quotations identify vehicles, quantities, specification, condition, price, tax treatment as applicable, deposits, delivery, payment milestones, warranty terms, service responsibilities, and quotation validity. TradeAI identifies whether an arrangement is outright purchase, finance introduced by an authorized party, or another service, and separates those obligations. TradeAI does not promise residual values, uptime, tax treatment, or delivery dates without a documented basis and approved authority.

## TradeAI fleet due diligence and delivery
TradeAI verifies business identity, authority of the purchasing representative, payment source, beneficial ownership where required, and any credit or fraud risks under its controls. TradeAI agrees registration, insurance, driver authorization, handover location, vehicle allocation, and acceptance criteria before dispatch. TradeAI captures per-vehicle condition, keys, documents, accessories, delivery evidence, and recipient acknowledgment. TradeAI reports shortages or damage promptly and agrees corrective action in writing.

## TradeAI account operations and aftercare
TradeAI maintains accurate fleet contact and vehicle records, handles defects and warranty referrals under applicable terms, and coordinates service or replacement discussions within the contracted scope. TradeAI escalates repeated faults, safety concerns, data requests, and service failures to accountable managers. TradeAI does not access driver data beyond agreed purpose and lawful authority. TradeAI provides clear invoice and payment reconciliation and records any approved commercial variation.

## TradeAI commercial governance
TradeAI approves discounts, credit terms, non-standard warranties, service-level commitments, and exclusivity only through documented delegated authority. TradeAI monitors account profitability, concentration, complaints, aged receivables, delivery performance, and customer outcomes. TradeAI reviews contracts on renewal and keeps executed agreements, schedules, approvals, and correspondence in controlled repositories.
