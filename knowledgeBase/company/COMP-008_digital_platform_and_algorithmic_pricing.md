## TradeAI digital platform and algorithmic pricing
TradeAI uses digital services for vehicle discovery, customer enquiries, stock records, pricing support, customer relationship management, and operational reporting. TradeAI systems are governed by access controls, data-quality rules, change approval, security monitoring, and documented ownership. TradeAI staff use approved accounts and devices and report suspected compromise, data loss, or inaccurate listings promptly.

## TradeAI vehicle listings and digital customer journey
TradeAI listings identify the correct vehicle, specification, mileage, condition, price, location, and material availability or delivery assumptions. TradeAI synchronizes online descriptions with verified stock records and removes or updates sold, reserved, or materially changed vehicles without undue delay. TradeAI labels images and vehicle features accurately and avoids misleading “from” pricing, unsupported claims, or undisclosed mandatory charges. TradeAI provides a route to ask questions and correct listing errors.

## TradeAI algorithmic pricing controls
TradeAI’s pricing tools, including the TradingAI Price Index referenced in supplied staff context, support but do not replace accountable human judgment. TradeAI pricing inputs may include comparable market evidence, age, mileage, specification, condition, reconditioning, location, demand, and stock strategy. TradeAI records material overrides and the reason for them. TradeAI tests data quality, stale inputs, anomalous outputs, and differential effects; TradeAI does not let an unreviewed model make a binding customer decision or a prohibited discriminatory inference.

## TradeAI transparency and human review
TradeAI staff explain the main evidence behind a vehicle price or part-exchange valuation in understandable terms and distinguish estimates from binding offers. TradeAI provides a meaningful human review channel when a customer disputes an automated or data-driven outcome. TradeAI monitors whether tool outputs create unfair, inconsistent, or unexplained outcomes and can pause a model or rule when a material risk is found.

## TradeAI privacy and cybersecurity
TradeAI collects only personal data needed for defined purposes, provides required privacy information, limits access by role, and shares data with lenders, suppliers, or service providers only under an appropriate lawful basis and approved process. TradeAI applies retention and deletion schedules, secure authentication, backups, patching, supplier review, and incident response. TradeAI does not put customer data in unapproved public tools or use it for a new purpose without authorization.

## TradeAI system governance and resilience
TradeAI assigns owners to critical systems, maintains change logs and recovery procedures, tests material releases, and evaluates third-party dependencies. TradeAI records defects, outages, security incidents, model versions, pricing policy changes, and approvals. TradeAI reviews platform controls regularly and after significant incidents, legal changes, or material system updates.
