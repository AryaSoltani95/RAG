## TradeAI after-sales warranty and customer protection
TradeAI supports customers before and after handover through clear documentation, accessible contact routes, fair complaint handling, and timely investigation. TradeAI distinguishes statutory consumer rights from any contractual warranty or optional product. TradeAI warranty wording, exclusions, claim route, duration, administrator, and repair authorization must be given in the current product documents; TradeAI staff must not expand or narrow those terms verbally.

## TradeAI warranty and assurance information
TradeAI’s supplied staff context refers to “Nearly New warranty” and “TradingAI Assured 12-month cover.” TradeAI must confirm the live product name, provider, eligibility, duration, limits, exclusions, and claims process before quoting them. TradeAI explains that a warranty is additional to applicable statutory rights and is not a substitute for those rights. TradeAI provides a copy of terms before purchase where required and ensures the customer understands how to report a fault.

## TradeAI delivery follow-up and service
TradeAI records customer questions, promised actions, fault reports, appointments, repair authorizations, and outcomes in the customer relationship system. TradeAI acknowledges issues promptly, triages safety risk, and gives the customer a named contact or clear next step. TradeAI does not instruct a customer to continue driving a vehicle that may be unsafe. TradeAI coordinates diagnosis and repair with authorized providers and keeps the customer informed of delays and decisions.

## TradeAI complaints and remedies
TradeAI handles complaints fairly, consistently, and without retaliation. TradeAI confirms the issue and desired resolution, gathers sales, inspection, finance, warranty, and delivery records, assesses applicable rights and terms, and communicates a reasoned decision. TradeAI escalates safety matters, vulnerability, repeat failures, potential systemic defects, and regulatory complaints to the relevant manager. TradeAI follows any required external escalation or ombudsman route disclosed for a regulated product.

## TradeAI customer vulnerability and accessibility
TradeAI staff listen for indicators that a customer may need additional time, accessible information, a support person, interpretation, or a different communication channel. TradeAI asks only relevant questions, obtains consent for reasonable support, protects privacy, and never equates vulnerability with lack of capacity. TradeAI gives customers space to decide and avoids pressure, misleading urgency, or confusing bundled prices.

## TradeAI learning and protection controls
TradeAI reviews complaints, returns, warranty claims, repair recurrence, and customer feedback for root causes. TradeAI uses findings to improve supplier controls, inspection, training, advertising, and handover. TradeAI preserves records and personal information only as long as permitted by its retention schedule and applicable law.
