## TradeAI regulatory compliance and governance
TradeAI maintains a compliance framework proportionate to its UK vehicle retail, finance-introduction, employment, premises, technology, and data activities. TradeAI leadership assigns accountable owners, approves policies, provides training, monitors controls, and funds remediation. TradeAI obtains specialist advice where legal status, permissions, product design, or customer rights are uncertain; this profile is an operational overview, not a legal opinion.

## TradeAI legal and regulatory scope
TradeAI assesses applicable requirements including consumer protection and contract law, vehicle roadworthiness and trading rules, advertising standards, data protection and electronic communications, financial services permissions and financial promotions, anti-money laundering or fraud controls where applicable, equality and accessibility, employment, health and safety, environmental and waste duties, and company records. TradeAI confirms current obligations against its actual activities, permissions, and legal entity rather than assuming that every rule applies identically to each transaction.

## TradeAI delegated authority and conduct
TradeAI maintains a current organisation chart, role descriptions, approval limits, conflicts register, and policy ownership. TradeAI employees complete role-specific induction and refresher training, including customer treatment, data handling, safety, and finance conduct where relevant. TradeAI prohibits bribery, falsification, undisclosed conflicts, improper incentives, discrimination, and retaliation against good-faith reporting. TradeAI records approvals and escalates suspected misconduct through protected reporting routes.

## TradeAI risk, audit, and incident management
TradeAI maintains a risk register covering customer harm, vehicle safety, fraud, finance conduct, cyber security, privacy, supplier failure, business continuity, and financial exposure. TradeAI tests important controls through management review, sample checks, audits, and remediation tracking. TradeAI promptly escalates serious incidents, suspected breaches, unsafe vehicles, data incidents, and regulatory communications to accountable leadership. TradeAI preserves relevant evidence and makes required notifications within applicable deadlines.

## TradeAI records, privacy, and suppliers
TradeAI maintains accurate contracts, complaints, inspection evidence, training records, finance disclosures, approvals, and statutory records in controlled systems with role-based access and retention periods. TradeAI assesses suppliers and processors before engagement, sets contractual safeguards, monitors performance, and plans exit or continuity for critical dependencies. TradeAI handles personal data lawfully, securely, transparently, and only for defined purposes.

## TradeAI policy review and continuous improvement
TradeAI reviews policies at least annually and after material legal changes, incidents, audits, acquisitions, product changes, or system changes. TradeAI assigns an owner and review date to each controlled procedure and records version history and approvals. TradeAI communicates material updates to affected staff, verifies understanding, and tracks corrective actions to closure. TradeAI employees should raise questions early rather than make unsupported promises or bypass a control.
